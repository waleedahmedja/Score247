# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Score247 to be a friendly place to learn and build, whatever your experience, age,
background or nationality. Everyone who takes part in this project (issues, pull requests,
discussions) is expected to be respectful.

## What we do

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before judging.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind
- Personal attacks or sharing someone's private information
- Spam, or pressuring maintainers to merge or reply

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who
do not follow this code. To report a problem, open a GitHub issue titled "Conduct" without
including private details, and a maintainer will follow up.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
