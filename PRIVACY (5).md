# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 25 October 2026*

Score247 is a scoring app for street cricket. It is built to work **entirely on your phone**.

## What Score247 collects

**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
It does not send any information to the developer or to any third party.

## What is stored on your phone

To let you carry on a match after closing the app, Score247 saves the following **only in the
app's private storage on your device**:

- the match you are playing (team names, player names, every ball scored, the result)
- your list of finished matches (used for History and player statistics)

Player and team names are whatever you type in. Other apps cannot read this data. You can delete
a finished match from its detail screen, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share as image** or **Share as text**, Score247 hands the picture or text to the
app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Instagram). From that moment, what happens to it is
governed by that app's own privacy policy. Score247 does not see where you send it. Sharing is
always your decision; nothing is shared automatically.

The picture contains a QR code and caption that point to the project's public GitHub page.

## Permissions

Score247 asks for no special permissions. It does not use your location, contacts, camera,
microphone or files.

## Children

Score247 collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will ship with the app and be shown in
**About and legal**, with a new date at the top.
